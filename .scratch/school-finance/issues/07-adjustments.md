# 07 — Adjustments

**What to build:** Admin-only per-student month adjustments: add an extra fee item to a student's month, or apply a waiver/discount reducing the charge. Adjustments appear on the student's account and are audited. Finance officer cannot make adjustments.

**Blocked by:** 06 — Monthly fee generation

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Admin can add an extra item to a student's month (increases their charge)
- [ ] Admin can apply a waiver/discount to a student's month (decreases their charge)
- [ ] Adjustments reflect on the student's balance immediately
- [ ] Adjustments are visible in the audit log
- [ ] Finance officer cannot adjust charges
